About Kay's Haven

We bring baby, children’s and teen essentials together in one place. Our stores are in Dzorwulu and Achimota, Ghana.

Frequently asked questions

Where do you deliver?

We arrange delivery across Ghana. Delivery timing and availability depend on the destination and will be confirmed with you.

How do I pay?

Online checkout is handled by Paystack and supports the payment channels available for your transaction, including card and mobile money. Kay's Haven does not receive or store your card details.

Do clothing items have size options?

Where a product has sizes, choose an available size on that product’s detail page before adding it to your cart.

Customer feedback

We welcome feedback about your shopping and delivery experience. Please send your comments through WhatsApp or email. We publish no customer quote or rating without permission.

Terms of sale

Product availability, price and displayed options are confirmed when your order is submitted. The total shown at checkout includes the displayed delivery fee. An order is accepted for processing after Paystack confirms payment. If an item is unavailable or an order cannot be fulfilled, we will contact you to arrange a refund or another option.

Please enter accurate contact and delivery details and keep your order reference. Product photos are illustrative; colour may vary slightly by screen.

Refunds and cancellations

Contact us as soon as possible with your order number if you need to cancel or change an order. We can cancel an order before it has been prepared or dispatched. Once dispatched, contact us to discuss a return; returned goods must be unused and in their original condition unless faulty or incorrect.

For an approved refund, Kay's Haven will initiate the refund through the original payment method where supported. Processing time depends on the payment provider and customer's bank or mobile-money provider. Contact us promptly if your order arrives damaged, incorrect or incomplete so we can resolve it.

Contact: Kayshaven.gh@gmail.com or WhatsApp 059 523 7037.

Delivery information

Nationwide delivery is available in Ghana. The current checkout delivery charge is GHS 15.00. Delivery timing depends on the delivery location and will be confirmed with the customer after ordering. Please provide a complete street address, city or town, region and a reachable phone number.

Store locations: Dzorwulu and Achimota. For delivery questions or to confirm an estimated arrival time, contact 059 523 7037.
